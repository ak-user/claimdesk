# Harbourline Mutual Group Benefits Booklet

> Fictional sample data for the ClaimDesk demo. Harbourline Mutual, Riverbend Logistics Ltd. and every person, clinic and number in this booklet are invented.

Plan sponsor: Riverbend Logistics Ltd. · Plan number: GB-20417 · Plan year: January 1 to December 31

## 1. About this booklet

### 1.1 Purpose

This booklet summarizes the group health and dental benefits that Harbourline Mutual provides to eligible employees of Riverbend Logistics Ltd. and their dependants. If this booklet and the master policy differ, the master policy applies.

### 1.2 Definitions

Plan year means January 1 to December 31. Eligible expense means a reasonable and customary charge for a covered service that is medically necessary and provided by a licensed or registered practitioner. Coinsurance is the percentage of an eligible expense that the plan pays; you pay the rest. Annual maximum is the most the plan will pay per person for a benefit in one plan year. Reimbursed amounts, not the amounts you were charged, count toward a maximum. Registered practitioner means a practitioner licensed or registered with the regulatory body for their profession in the province where the service is provided.

## 2. Eligibility and enrolment

### 2.1 Who is eligible

Permanent employees who work at least 24 hours per week are eligible after completing 3 months of continuous employment. Contract and seasonal workers are not eligible.

### 2.2 Dependants

You can cover your spouse and your unmarried dependent children. Children are covered to age 21, or to age 25 if they are full-time students at an accredited school. A child with a disability that began before the age limit stays covered while the disability continues.

### 2.3 When coverage ends

Coverage ends on the earliest of: the date your employment ends, the date you stop meeting the eligibility rules, or the date the group policy ends. Dependant coverage ends when your coverage ends or when the dependant no longer qualifies.

## 3. Submitting a claim

### 3.1 How to submit

Submit claims through the Harbourline member portal or mobile app. Upload a clear photo or PDF of an itemized receipt. Keep your original receipts for 12 months after you submit a claim, because claims may be selected for audit.

### 3.2 Claim deadline

Submit each claim within 12 months of the date of service. Claims received more than 12 months after the date of service are not eligible for payment. If your coverage ends, you have 90 days after the end date to submit claims for services received while you were covered.

### 3.3 Required information

Every claim must show the member ID, the patient's name, the date of service, the type of service, the amount charged, and the provider's name. Claims for paramedical services (physiotherapy, massage therapy, chiropractic care and psychology) must also show the practitioner's registration number. Claims without the required information are returned for correction.

### 3.4 Processing time

Most complete claims are processed within 5 business days. A claims examiner reviews every claim before payment, and the plan may ask for more information or a referral before deciding.

## 4. Health benefits

### 4.1 Prescription drugs

The plan pays 80% of the cost of eligible prescription drugs that require a prescription by law. Dispensing fees are covered up to $10 per prescription. If a generic equivalent exists, reimbursement is based on the lowest-cost generic unless your doctor writes "no substitution" for a medical reason. There is no annual maximum for prescription drugs.

### 4.2 Physiotherapy

The plan pays 80% of eligible physiotherapy expenses, up to an annual maximum of $750 per person per plan year. Treatment must be provided by a registered physiotherapist. A physician's referral is not required. Assessments and treatment sessions count toward the same maximum.

### 4.3 Massage therapy

The plan pays 80% of eligible massage therapy expenses, up to an annual maximum of $500 per person per plan year. Treatment must be provided by a registered massage therapist. The plan covers one treatment per day.

### 4.4 Chiropractic care

The plan pays 80% of eligible chiropractic expenses, up to an annual maximum of $500 per person per plan year. X-rays taken by a chiropractor are covered once per plan year within this maximum.

### 4.5 Psychology and counselling

The plan pays 80% of eligible fees for a registered psychologist, registered social worker or registered psychotherapist, up to an annual maximum of $1,000 per person per plan year.

### 4.6 Vision care

The plan pays 100% of the cost of prescription eyeglasses or contact lenses, up to $250 per person in any 24-month period. Laser eye surgery is covered within the same $250 maximum. Eye exams are covered once every 24 months, up to $100 per exam.

## 5. Dental benefits

### 5.1 Basic services

The plan pays 80% of eligible basic dental services: exams and cleanings once every 9 months, X-rays, fillings, extractions and root canals.

### 5.2 Major services

The plan pays 50% of eligible major dental services: crowns, bridges, dentures and implants. Major services are covered only after you have been enrolled in the plan for 12 months.

### 5.3 Dental annual maximum

Basic and major dental services share one annual maximum of $1,500 per person per plan year.

### 5.4 Fee guide

Dental expenses are reimbursed up to the current dental fee guide for general practitioners in the province where you live. If your dentist charges more than the fee guide, you pay the difference.

## 6. Exclusions

The plan does not cover: services covered by a provincial health plan or a workers' compensation board; cosmetic procedures; missed appointment fees; form completion fees; services provided by a member of your immediate family; drugs or treatments that are experimental; and expenses for which you are not legally required to pay.

## 7. Coordination of benefits

If you or your dependants are covered by another group plan, submit claims to both plans to receive up to 100% of eligible expenses. Submit your own claims to this plan first. For a child, submit first to the plan of the parent whose birthday comes earlier in the calendar year.

## 8. Appeals

If you disagree with a claim decision, send a written appeal within 90 days of the decision date. Include the claim number, the reason for the appeal, and any new supporting documents. A different examiner reviews each appeal.

## 9. Privacy

Harbourline Mutual collects, uses and discloses personal information only to administer the plan, assess claims, prevent fraud, and meet legal requirements. Access to your information is limited to staff who need it for these purposes.
